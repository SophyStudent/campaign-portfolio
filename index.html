About Me

I work as an independent contractor within agency teams and directly with businesses, contributing to digital marketing strategy, campaign execution, technical implementation, and performance analysis.

My work spans the full digital marketing process: understanding the market, identifying opportunities, reaching the right audiences, developing and evaluating creative strategies, implementing conversion tracking, analyzing results, and improving campaign performance.

I also work on the technical side of digital visibility, building and structuring websites, landing pages, and web applications to ensure that a business's digital platforms are properly organized, accessible, search-friendly, and aligned with its marketing objectives.

This combination allows me to approach digital marketing from both the promotional and technical perspectives.

What I Would Do for Your Show

My approach would combine audience research, paid advertising, creative analysis, tracking, and performance optimization to build awareness and grow an engaged audience for the show.

  1. 1

    Research the Market & Identify Opportunities

    I would research comparable reality shows, entertainment campaigns, competing content, and relevant hair and beauty brands to understand their advertising approaches, audience engagement, and positioning.

    The goal would be to identify opportunities for the show to stand out and develop a clear promotional angle.

  2. 2

    Implement Tracking Before Advertising Spend

    Before launching campaigns, I would establish the measurement infrastructure.

    This would include:

    • Meta Pixel and Conversions API.
    • Google Analytics 4.
    • Google Tag Manager.
    • UTM campaign parameters.
    • Relevant website and landing-page conversion events.

    This ensures campaign traffic, engagement, and agreed conversion actions can be measured.

  3. 3

    Build Relevant Audiences

    Potential audience segments would include:

    • Hair enthusiasts.
    • Hairstylists and salon owners.
    • Beauty and lifestyle audiences.
    • Reality television and entertainment fans.
    • People interested in the show's themes and participants.

    I would also develop lookalike audiences where sufficient first-party data is available and retarget people who have watched promotional videos, visited the website, or engaged with campaign content.

  4. 4

    Give Each Advertising Platform a Clear Role

    TikTok and Instagram: Short-form clips, engaging moments, participant introductions, behind-the-scenes content, and audience interaction.
    YouTube: Trailers, longer promotional videos, and video-view campaigns.
    Google Search: Reaching people actively searching for the show, its participants, or related topics.
    Programmatic Advertising: Expanding reach through relevant audience and contextual targeting where appropriate.

    Platform selection and budget allocation would depend on the campaign objectives, available assets, audience research, and expected costs.

  5. 5

    Analyze and Test the Creative

    I would study the available promotional content, evaluate its messaging and appeal, and recommend creative angles, hooks, and formats suited to the intended audiences.

    Different approaches would be tested using available video and image assets. Campaign performance would then inform which creative directions should receive more investment and which should be adjusted.

  6. 6

    Measure and Report Performance

    I would develop a centralized reporting dashboard covering agreed campaign metrics, such as:

    Awareness

    • Reach
    • Impressions
    • Video views
    • Cost per thousand impressions

    Engagement

    • Watch time
    • Video completion rate
    • Shares
    • Comments
    • Engagement rate

    Audience Growth

    • New followers
    • Returning viewers
    • Audience growth rate

    Conversions

    • Website visits
    • Mailing-list registrations
    • Sign-ups
    • Ticket sales or other agreed actions, where applicable

    The reporting structure would be based on the show's primary objectives, rather than treating every metric as equally important.

What I Would Need From You

To develop the campaign properly, I would need:

  • Details about the show, its concept, format, and launch schedule.
  • The target audience and geographical markets.
  • Available promotional videos, photographs, trailers, and other creative assets.
  • Existing social media accounts and website information.
  • The proposed advertising budget.
  • The primary campaign objective: views, followers, registrations, sign-ups, ticket sales, or another measurable outcome.

From there, I can develop a campaign strategy, advertising structure, creative testing plan, tracking requirements, and reporting framework.
